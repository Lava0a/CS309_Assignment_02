Flexible navigation

The header is a flex container. The logo and links are pushed apart with justify-content.

Grid content

Cards sit in three equal columns on wide screens. The gap property handles all the spacing.

Two breakpoints

The grid has one column on phones, two on tablets (640px and up) and three on desktops (1024px and up).

About this page

This wide card spans every column. All colors, the radius and the spacing come from CSS variables defined once on :root.
